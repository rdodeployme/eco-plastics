§ 01The opportunity

Bins, bumpers and pallets are the easy wins.

Some of the most recyclable plastics there are, and too many of them still end up in landfill.

  1. Big and rigidHeavy, solid items. Easy to identify, worth collecting in bulk.
  2. One polymer eachA wheelie bin is HDPE. A bumper bar is PP. A pallet is one or the other.
  3. Closed loopBin back to bin. Pallet back to pallet.

§ 03Why separation wins

Both float.

A float–sink tank sorts plastics by density. HDPE and PP are both lighter than water, so both rise to the top.3

§ 03Why separation wins

Mixed, they're worth less. So they never meet.

  1. 01Segregated at the source
  2. 02Carried separately
  3. 03Processed in separate batches
  4. 04Tracked, batch by batch
