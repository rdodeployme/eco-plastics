02 In and out

Where it comes from. What it becomes.

Comes from

  • Pallet-pool and logistics operators
  • Food and beverage
  • Manufacturers
  • Warehouses

Comes off

  • Steel reinforcing rods (in some pallets)
  • RFID tags
  • Labels

Becomes

  • New pallets
  • Crates
  • Construction products

02 Its path through the line Proposed

Eight steps, in its own lane.

  1. 01Receive and weighEach load weighed in and logged against its stream.
  2. 02Inspect and sortChecked by stream, and by polymer for pallets.
  3. 03StripRods, tags and labels come off.
  4. 04ShredCut down to flake.
  5. 05WashDirt, labels and residue washed off.
  6. 06Float–sinkAnything heavier than water drops out.
  7. 07DryFlake dried, ready to bag.
  8. 08Bag or pelletBagged as flake, or extruded to pellet.
Two polymers, one shape

Pallets look alike but come in HDPE or PP. Keeping them apart is what keeps the flake clean.
