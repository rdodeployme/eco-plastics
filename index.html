Why we exist

In 2023–24, Australia recovered about 14% of the plastic that reached end of life. The other 86% wasn’t. Rigid plastics like bins, crates, pallets and pipe are some of the easiest to recycle, and too many still go to landfill. We recycle them.

Recovery figure: Australian Plastics Flows and Fates 2023–24, as reported by WMRR (2025).

How it works

One call. We do the rest.

  1. 01Call or email

    Tell us what you’ve got, roughly how much, and where it is.

  2. 02We arrange it

    We confirm what we can take and arrange collection or delivery to North Geelong.

  3. 03Kept separate

    Each stream and polymer runs on its own, so nothing gets mixed.

  4. 04Recycled

    Rigid plastics are stripped, shredded and washed into clean flake. Pallet wrap is baled for film recyclers.
