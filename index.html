00 The short version

In 2023–24, Australia recovered about 14% of the plastic that reached end of life. The other 86% wasn’t. Wheelie bins, bumper bars and pallets are the easiest place to change that. Eco Plastics is being built to do it, inside a site that already runs.

Recovery figure: Australian Plastics Flows and Fates 2023–24, as reported by WMRR (2025).
