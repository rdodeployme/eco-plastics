03 Three states and the ACT

Four places. One site.

What landfill costs in each, and how your plastic gets to us.

New South Wales

$180.20

Waste levy, metro, a tonne, from 1 July 20265

NSW loads come down the Hume Highway, straight to our site.

Victoria

$177.19

Waste levy, metro, a tonne, from 1 July 20266

The shortest run we have. Victorian loads come straight in.

Australian Capital Territory

$210.90

Landfill fee, commercial waste, a tonne, from 1 July 20268

Canberra loads head out to the Hume and come down with the NSW loads.

South Australia

$171.00

Waste levy, metro Adelaide, a tonne, from 1 July 20267

SA loads cross the border on the Western Highway and come in from the west.

Our own trucks.
