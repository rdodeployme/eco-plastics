What we take/Plastic crates

Plastic crate recycling.

HDPEPPHigh-density polyethylene or polypropylene Resin code 2 or 5

Broken and retired crates from growers, bakeries, dairies and distribution centres. We sort them into HDPE and PP and recycle each on its own.

What it is
Produce, bread, dairy and distribution crates. Each load is sorted by polymer.
Who it’s for
Supermarket distribution centres, growers, bakeries, dairies and logistics operators.

01 In and out

Where it comes from. What it becomes.

Comes from

  • Distribution centres
  • Growers and packers
  • Bakeries and dairies
  • Logistics operators

Comes off

  • Labels and barcodes
  • Metal clips or pins (some crates)
  • Produce residue

Can go into

  • New crates
  • Pallets
  • Bins

Mixed-colour flake. Illustrative.

01 Its path through the line

Eight steps, in its own lane.

  1. 01Receive and checkEach load checked in against its product.
  2. 02Inspect and sortChecked, and sorted into HDPE and PP.
  3. 03StripLabels, clips and pins come off.
  4. 04ShredCut down to flake.
  5. 05WashDirt, labels and residue washed off.
  6. 06Float–sinkAnything heavier than water drops out.
  7. 07DryFlake dried, ready to bag.
  8. 08BagBagged as clean flake, ready for manufacturers.
Two polymers, one shape

Crates come in HDPE or PP. Keeping them apart is what keeps the flake clean.

Pooled crates

Crates owned by a pooling company belong to that company. Send us your own, or ones the owner has written off.
