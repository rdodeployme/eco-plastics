What we take/Poly pipe

Poly pipe recycling.

HDPEHigh-density polyethylene Resin code 2

Offcuts, coils and old poly pipe from civil, plumbing and irrigation work. We recycle the HDPE; fittings, tape and dirt come off on our line.

What it is
Black poly pipe and offcuts from water, irrigation, gas and civil work.
Who it’s for
Civil contractors, plumbers, irrigation installers, farms and water authorities.

01 Its path through the line

Eight steps, in its own lane.

  1. 01Receive and checkEach load checked in against its product.
  2. 02Inspect and sortChecked by product. Anything that isn’t the right plastic comes out.
  3. 03StripFittings and tape come off.
  4. 04ShredCut down to flake.
  5. 05WashDirt, labels and residue washed off.
  6. 06Float–sinkAnything heavier than water drops out.
  7. 07DryFlake dried, ready to bag.
  8. 08BagBagged as clean flake, ready for manufacturers.
Tell us the size

Tell us the diameter and roughly how many metres or coils, so we can plan the load.

Polyethylene only

We take poly pipe only. White or grey PVC pipe is a different plastic.
