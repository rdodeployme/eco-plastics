05 Tracking

Every load, on the record.

The Nunjara load register logs every inbound load, and it’s the model for how Eco Plastics will track every batch.

  • Company, truck registration, origin, truck configuration, volume and material.
  • Photos of the load and the receipt.
  • Locked records carry a SHA-256 hash. Originals are kept; corrections are appended.
  • Operator and device ID on every record.
  • Works offline and syncs to the office.
