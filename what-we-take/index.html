01 What comes out

What it comes out as.

Rigid plastics leave the line as regrind: shredded, granulated and packed in bulk bags. Pallet wrap leaves as baled film.

HDPE

HDPE regrind

Resin code 2 · 0.94–0.97 g/cm³

From

  • Wheelie bins
  • Commercial bins
  • Plastic pallets
  • IBC bottles
  • Plastic crates
  • Milk crates
  • Poly pipe

Can go into

New bins, pipe, pallets and crates.

PP

PP regrind

Resin code 5 · 0.90–0.91 g/cm³

From

  • Car bumper bars
  • Car plastics
  • Plastic pallets
  • Plastic crates
  • Shopping baskets
  • Plastic chairs

Can go into

Automotive and industrial mouldings, crates and pallets.

PE

Rotomoulded PE regrind

Resin code 2 or 4 · lighter than water

From

  • Tanks and barriers

Can go into

Pipe, pallets, and garden and building products.

LLDPE

Baled film

Resin code 4 · baled, not shredded

From

  • Pallet wrap

Can go into

Sent to film recyclers for new film, bin liners and bags.

01 What we don’t take

Some things we can’t accept.

  • Asbestos
  • Prescribed waste
  • Hazardous chemicals
  • Firearms or explosives
  • Containers or tanks that held chemicals, fuel or oil
