What we take/Wheelie bins

Wheelie bin recycling.

HDPEHigh-density polyethylene Resin code 2

Broken, faded and replaced wheelie bins. We recycle the HDPE body and lid, and the wheels and axles come off on our line.

What it is
Injection-moulded and UV-stabilised.
Who it’s for
Councils running bin replacement programs, bin makers and waste contractors.

01 In and out

Where it comes from. What it becomes.

Comes from

  • Councils, through bin replacement programs
  • Bin makers
  • Waste contractors

Comes off

  • Steel axles
  • Wheels (rubber or PP)
  • Food and waste residue

Can go into

  • New bins
  • Pipe
  • Pallets
  • Crates

HDPE flake. Illustrative.

01 Its path through the line

Eight steps, in its own lane.

  1. 01Receive and checkEach load checked in against its product.
  2. 02Inspect and sortChecked by product. Anything that isn’t the right plastic comes out.
  3. 03StripAxles and wheels come off.
  4. 04ShredCut down to flake.
  5. 05WashDirt, labels and residue washed off.
  6. 06Float–sinkAnything heavier than water drops out.
  7. 07DryFlake dried, ready to bag.
  8. 08BagBagged as clean flake, ready for manufacturers.
Colour

Mixed lid colours make a dark regrind. Dark products, like bin bodies, pipe and pallets, don’t mind.
